YOUR COURIER HANDBOOK

Know the route. Win the shift.

  1. Pair up. Create two accounts in separate browser sessions. One courier creates a room; the other joins with its code. Both click Ready.
  2. Move and collect. Use WASD or arrow keys. Walk over animated gold parcels to collect them. Carry up to three at once.
  3. Make the delivery. Walk into your own colored delivery desk. Your bag is deposited automatically for one point per parcel.
  4. Make a little trouble. Space dashes in your movement direction (or the direction you last faced). A dash hit stuns your rival briefly and drops one of their parcels. Dash recharges in three seconds.
  5. Mind the campus traffic. Robots stun you and drop one parcel on contact. A short immunity window prevents repeated hits. Collect a green speed boost for five seconds of faster movement; it respawns after twelve seconds.
  6. Take the win. Reach 12 points first, or lead when the three-minute timer ends. Equal scores are a draw. A player who leaves an active match forfeits; a connection loss has a 15-second reconnection window.
  7. Try cheat mode. Press C to toggle increased speed, robot and dash-hit immunity, and 12 points per delivered parcel. Press C again to restore normal abilities. Any match that used cheating is marked as practice and excluded from cumulative rankings. A new match resets cheating.

To test two accounts on one computer, use a normal window and a private window, or two different browsers. Tabs in the same browser share a login session.
